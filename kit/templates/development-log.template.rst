Development log
===============

What was done, and why, newest first. The plan is ``docs/backlog``. An entry says what changed, why, the evidence, what was considered and rejected, and the mistakes.
*Dates are today's date from the clock, read when the entry is written (never counted forward).*

YYYY-MM-DD: what this entry is about
------------------------------------

**What.** <The change, in a few plain sentences.>

**Why.** <What was found, and by what: a guard, a read, a scan, a measure.>

**Evidence.** <What was checked: the real output before and after, the tests, the mutation that showed a new guard bites.>

**Considered and rejected.** <The alternative, and the reason, so nobody re-tries it.>

**Mistakes.** <One line each, and what each taught.>
