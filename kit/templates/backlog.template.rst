Backlog
=======

The plan, as a table: one row per item, with a status and the reason it is on the list. An item that is decided *not* to be built stays, with the reason, so it is not proposed again.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 5 40 15 40

   * - #
     - Item
     - Status
     - Why, or why not
   * - 1
     - <what>
     - open
     - <what it would give, and what it costs>
