The playbook
============

Read in order, or jump to what you need. The one document an agent needs is ``kit/AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.rst``; these chapters give the reasons and the stories behind each rule.

- `The idea <01-the-idea.rst>`__: every layer is the top of the layers beneath it, and why that is worth the work.
- `The loop <02-the-loop.rst>`__: one iteration, and the standing orders that keep an agent doing it well.
- `Guards <03-guards.rst>`__: rules as tests, debts as data, proving a guard bites, the cold read as a test.
- `Tools <04-tools.rst>`__: what was automated, why, and how to rebuild it for another language.
- `Reading <05-reading.rst>`__: finding what no guard measures, and when to stop.
- `CI and releases <06-ci-and-releases.rst>`__: measure first, what paid, what did not, and releasing in batches.
- `Words and output <07-words-and-output.rst>`__: the rules for what a person reads.
- `Lessons <08-lessons.rst>`__: every mistake, and the rule each became.
- `Starting a project <09-starting-a-project.rst>`__: the order of work, in any language.
- `How close are we? <10-how-close-are-we.rst>`__: the numbers, and the plain words.
- `The story <11-the-story.rst>`__: how it was actually done, in order.
